In Germany, all persons who rent an accommodation, as well as all persons who will be residing in Germany for more than three months, are obligated to register their address. Within two weeks after your arrival in Cottbus or Senftenberg, you and your family members must register your address at the City Administration Office (Stadtbüro).

## Required Documents

To register, you will need:

1. **Confirmation from your landlord (Wohnungsgeberbestätigung)** - Your landlord must complete and sign this form
2. **Your passport** - Valid identification document
3. **German Bank Account** - If not available, provide a Blocked Account Statement (Sperrkontoauszug)

## Registration Process

At the city office in Cottbus, you need to:

1. Take a number from the machine in the waiting area
2. Wait until your number is called
3. Present your documents to the registration clerk
4. Complete the registration form (Anmeldung)

**Please note:** On some days, you may have to wait up to two hours until your number is called. Please make sure you have enough time and schedule your visit accordingly.

## Online Appointment Booking (Recommended)

The most convenient option is to book an appointment online in advance:

- **Check current wait times:** [Aktuelle Wartesituation](https://www.cottbus.de/verwaltung/gb_ii/buergerservice/stadtbuero/aktuelle_wartesituation.html)
- **Book an appointment:** [Online Appointment Booking](https://www.cottbus.de/stadtverwaltung/d31/buergerservice/stadtbuero/moeglichkeiten_zur_terminvereinbarung.html)

Booking online helps you avoid long wait times and ensures that you can complete your registration quickly.

## Important Notes

- Registration is **mandatory** - failing to register can result in fines
- You must register within **14 days** of moving into your accommodation
- Bring all required documents - missing documents will delay your registration
- German-speaking companions are helpful but not required; many offices have English-speaking staff
- Keep a copy of your registration certificate (Meldebescheinigung) for future use

## After Registration

Once registered, you will receive a **Meldebescheinigung (Certificate of Registration)**, which is required for:
- Opening a German bank account
- University enrollment/registration
- Applying for health insurance
- Obtaining a German tax identification number
