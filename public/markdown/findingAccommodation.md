The most frequently asked question after getting a Letter of Admission is regarding accommodation. There are quite a lot of cost-effective options available for students near the university and market area. All the accommodation options along with their proximity to the University are as follows:


#### 1. Studentenwerk Frankfurt (Oder)
There are 4 Halls of Residence (Studentenwohnheim) in Cottbus at different locations. The typology of the rooms varies from single-room apartments, 2-shared apartments to 5-shared apartments. In the shared apartments, the toilet and kitchen are common to use among the occupants and the bedrooms are separate.

​The Halls of Residence are located at:

- Universitätsstraße
- Erich-Weinert Straße
- Lipezker Straße
- Potsdamer Straße

The rooms are furnished and provided with a mattress. Furniture may slightly vary according to the building. LAN is available at Universitätsstraße, Erich Weinert Straße and Lipezker Straße, while Potsdamer Straße does not have Internet facility.

Office: Studentenhaus, Universitätsstraße 20, 03046 Cottbus   


#### 2. Guesthouse der Uni-Service GmbH
Located near the Central Campus, the Guesthouse is a good option for those looking for accommodation for the short or long term. It is on the route of Bus no. 16, and the nearest bus stop is Papitzer Straße.

Internet facilities are available on request and on an additional payment of EUR 20.


Email: info@gaestehaus-uni.de

Office: Guesthouse der Uni GmbH, Papitzer Straße 4, 03046 Cottbus


#### 3. T-1 Campus Cottbus
The T1 Campus Cottbus is also located near the central campus of BTU. It offers living comfort in 313 apartments, which are tailor-made for student life. T1 Campus Cottbus also offers space for studying in groups and leisure activities, with a cafeteria with outdoor seating and rooms for collective studying. The student apartments are either 1-bedroom or 2-bedroom units.

Email: studenten@t1-cottbus.de

Office: T1 Campus, Jamlitzer Straße 9, 03046 Cottbus


#### 4. Shared Private Accommodation
If you decide to stay in private shared accommodation, you can look for one on Wg-gesucht.de. These are mostly furnished apartments shared between 2-4 people, with a common kitchen and bathroom but private bedrooms. Generally, landlords ask to meet tenants personally, so you should ideally be there in person; however, some landlords agree to conduct a video call. Be cautious and do not transfer any deposits to the landlord before confirming with someone here that the property is legitimate.
