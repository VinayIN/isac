The closest international airports to BTU are Berlin Brandenburg Airport (BER). The German airport with the most international connections is Frankfurt/Main (FRA). If you are flying to Germany, you must take the train to Cottbus and further to Senftenberg. There is no university service to pick up students; you need to reach your accommodation on your own. Below, we have provided some tips and directions that could be helpful.

*In case you need assistance urgently, do reach us through [Instagram](https://instagram.com/isac_cottbus)*



### Landing in Berlin Airport
Look for signs overhead that will guide you towards the train platform.

**NOTE:** Tickets, once purchased, are valid throughout the day unless used. Do not panic and don't rush to catch the train.

You can buy a ticket for the bus or train at any ticket machine. Please be aware that you must stamp your ticket upon entering the bus or on the platform if you are taking the train. There will be no person to stamp your ticket; instead, use the machines available.

You can use any intermediate station like **Berlin Ostkreuz**, **Berlin Hauptbahnhof**, **Berlin Ostbahnhof**, or **Königs Wusterhausen** before reaching Cottbus. You should then be able to take the RE 2 train to Cottbus, which departs every hour from these intermediate stations. 

If you are traveling to Senftenberg, you can take the RB49 or RE18 regional trains, which depart from Cottbus main station every hour.

### Landing in Frankfurt Main 
At the airport, several regional trains (S8, S9) departing from Platform Regio 1 can take you directly to Frankfurt main train station. From Frankfurt main station, trains to Leipzig depart every 1-2 hours, where you can change to board another train to Cottbus or Senftenberg. The journey from Frankfurt to Cottbus or Senftenberg via Leipzig takes approximately six hours.

There is also an ICE train that runs directly from the long-distance train tracks to Leipzig Hbf or Berlin Hbf every hour.


### From Cottbus to Your Accommodation
Once you have arrived by train in Cottbus or Senftenberg, you can take various buses (or in Cottbus you can also use the tram) to reach your accommodation.
- Bus 16 takes you to the main Cottbus campus
- Bus 47 takes you to IKMZ (Cottbus Library)
- All trams stop at Stadthalle. This is a major hub from where all transport vehicles can interchange routes

You can find more information, including the bus line numbers you should take, by visiting the Berlin-Brandenburg Transportation Authority's website or the DB Navigator.
