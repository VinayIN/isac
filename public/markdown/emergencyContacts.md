## Useful Information for Emergencies
1. Know your address in German
2. Have your passport and student ID readily available
3. Keep emergency contact numbers saved in your phone
4. Register with your national embassy for emergency assistance
5. Keep contact information of trusted friends and family members from home

## Public Holidays in Germany/Brandenburg
- **January 1:** New Year's Day
- **Good Friday:** Before Easter
- **Easter Sunday and Easter Monday:** Spring holiday
- **May 1:** International Workers' Day
- **Ascension Day:** 39 days after Easter
- **Whit Sunday and Whit Monday:** 49-50 days after Easter
- **October 3:** German Unification Day
- **October 31:** Reformation Day (Brandenburg)
- **December 25-26:** Christmas Day and Boxing Day
